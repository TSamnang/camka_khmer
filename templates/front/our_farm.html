Our process

How We Verify Every Farm

Not just anyone can join ចម្ការខ្មែរ. Every partner farm goes through a rigorous review to ensure quality and honesty.

Shop Their Products
1
Application & Interview

Farmers apply online or through our field agents. We conduct a Khmer-language interview to understand their land, methods, and crops.

2
On-Site Farm Visit

Our team physically visits every farm before onboarding. We assess soil health, water source, storage conditions, and farming practices.

3
Product Sampling

We sample produce and test for chemical residues, freshness, and quality. Only farms that pass are listed on ចម្ការខ្មែរ.

4
Annual Re-Verification

Each farm is re-visited every year. We share buyer feedback and work with farmers on continuous improvement.
